Privacy

Privacy policy

Boards is a project and task-management app for Root communities. Last updated: 3 October 2026.

Information used

Boards uses information supplied by Root about your community and its members, including names, profile pictures and member IDs. It uses this information to identify the signed-in member, show people with access to the app and assign work.

Boards stores the workspace content people add, such as projects, tasks, sections, assignments, dates, priorities, subtasks, dependencies, comments, @mentions, likes, templates, project settings and uploaded-file details. Comments and assignments are used to show relevant notifications inside Boards. Boards does not send these notifications to Root's own notification feed.

When a file is uploaded through Root, its contents are stored as a Root asset. Boards stores the asset reference and available file details, such as name, size and file type, in the workspace. Boards accepts raster images, PDFs, DOCX, PPTX and XLSX files; other types are rejected by Boards. Root's picker transfers a file before Boards validates it, so a rejected upload may remain as an unlinked Root asset. Root controls the retention of such assets.

Storage and sharing

Workspace information is stored using Root's app-data service and shared with authorised users of the app in the same community. Boards also keeps a browser-local copy of workspace information; the light/dark theme preference is saved in that browser. Root processes information as part of providing its platform and services.

Boards does not sell workspace information or use it for advertising.

Retention and control

Archived projects remain in the workspace until restored or moved to Trash. Items moved to Trash—including projects and their contents, sections and their tasks, tasks, attachments, comments, subtasks and templates—can be restored or permanently deleted. Trash items are retained for up to 30 days and are removed from Boards when the workspace is next loaded or while the app is open after that period, unless permanently deleted sooner.

For an attachment in Trash, Boards retains the Root asset reference during the Trash period. Permanently deleting the Trash item or its expiry removes the Boards reference. Root controls the storage and retention of the underlying asset, so removing its reference from Boards does not guarantee immediate erasure of the file from Root's asset storage.

For help with access to your community workspace or a privacy request, contact your Root community administrator.
